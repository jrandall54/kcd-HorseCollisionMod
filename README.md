# Horse Collision Mod for Kingdom Come: Deliverance

Re-enables physics-based horse collision in Kingdom Come: Deliverance.

By default, KCD disabled horse collision in towns to prevent accidental crime and reputation loss. This mod safely restores the ability to knock over and ragdoll NPCs when riding your horse at high speeds.

## Features
- Dynamic, physics-accurate ragdolling of NPCs when hit by a horse at speed.
- Fully togglable Crime and Reputation system (currently defaulted to OFF, meaning no bounty for accidentally running over a guard).
- Configurable speed thresholds and damage multipliers.

## Installation
Drop HorseCollisionMod_v1.0.zip into your mod manager (Vortex) or extract it directly into your Kingdom Come - Deliverance/Mods directory.
