HorseCollisionMod

version 2.0.0 · jrandall54

Horse Collision Overhaul for Kingdom Come: Deliverance.

Gives mounted collisions a reaction proportional to the horse's gait. In vanilla, riding into an NPC produces only an audio bark and no animation at any speed. This module adds three tiers:

  • Walk - the NPC plays a full standing stagger animation and is not

    • knocked down. No health damage, and the vanilla bark still fires.

  • Trot - a physics ragdoll at reduced impulse.
  • Gallop - a physics ragdoll at full impulse.

At trot and gallop the horse also loses stamina, and dismounts Henry when spent. That is the anti-bulldozing budget: riding through a crowd has to end with Henry on the ground rather than being a free way to scatter a dozen people.

How the walk stagger works

This part has no precedent in other KCD mods, so it is worth stating plainly. Lua cannot drive an NPC's animation directly. Everything obvious was tried and fails silently: human:PlayAnim is accepted but never renders, physics impulses are ignored because actors are animation-driven, soul:DealDamage only moves stat numbers, and the hitReaction and combat:hit brain messages reach a behavior tree that cannot drive the body.

What does work is actor:StartInteractiveActionByName, the call vanilla uses to make an actor mime opening a door. It takes over the body, plays a whole animation, and hands control back. Its name argument resolves against the FragTags of exactly one Mannequin fragment, AnimationControlled, where vanilla ships only object interactions.

So the mod adds its own entries there. Four data files ship alongside this script, all generated by build_adb.py, and all four are required:

  • kcd_male_database.adb - adds hcm_stagger_* options pointing at

    • hitreaction_idle_medium_torso_stab_*, a standing hit reaction the game already contains.

  • kcd_animationControlledTags.xml - declares those FragTags. An option

    • whose tag is not declared aborts after one frame.

  • wh_female_fragmentids.xml - declares AnimationControlled for women,

    • who have the same clips but no such fragment in vanilla.

  • wh_female_database.adb - the fragment block itself, added wholesale.

See docs/TESTING_DIARY.md for the full investigation.

Limitations

  • Dogs and other animals do not react. They use separate animation

    • databases which this mod does not touch.

  • Ships full replacements of the male and female animation databases, so

    • it conflicts with any mod that edits human animations.

IsInHorseFootprint(npc, horsePos, horseForward, speed)

Tests whether a victim is actually under the horse.

The sphere search is a broad-phase cull and nothing more. A horse is about two meters long and under a meter wide, so a sphere around its origin also catches people walking alongside or trailing behind it, which is what makes collisions feel like they reach too far.

This narrows the sphere to an oriented box: the victim must be within the horse's width laterally, between its rear and front reach along its facing, and at a comparable height. The front reach is extended by the distance the horse travels in one tick so that fast victims are not missed between frames.

ParameterTypeDescription
npctablevictim entity
horsePostableworld position of the horse
horseForwardtablehorse facing, unit vector
speednumberhorse speed in meters per second

Returns boolean true when the victim is inside the footprint

GetImpactDir(npc, velocity, speed)

Works out which side of the victim the impact lands on.

The stagger clips are authored relative to the NPC's facing, so the impact has to be expressed in the victim's own frame. Using the horse's frame instead makes NPCs stagger into the horse rather than away from it.

The impact is derived from the horse's direction of travel, not its position. Position is ambiguous at the moment of contact, when the horse is effectively on top of the victim and a centimeter either way flips the answer. Travel direction is stable, and it is what actually decides which side of the body is struck: a horse moving north hits the south face of whoever is in front of it.

ParameterTypeDescription
npctablevictim entity
velocitytablehorse velocity vector
speednumberhorse speed in meters per second

Returns string one of "so_forward", "so_back", "so_left", "so_right"

PlayStagger(npc, velocity, speed)

Plays the walk-speed stagger on a victim.

Calls actor:StartInteractiveActionByName with one of the hcm_stagger_* names this mod adds to the animation database. See the module header for why this is the only call that works.

The return value reports whether the call was accepted, which is not the same as the animation playing: an unrecognized name is accepted and then aborts within a frame. Only in-game observation confirms playback.

Used at trot and gallop. actor:Fall switches the victim to a ragdoll, after which an impulse can be applied. Impulses are ignored on an upright, animation-driven actor, which is why the order matters and why the impulse is deferred by a tick.

IsCombatCollision(npc)

Whether this collision should count as a combat one.

Two independent signals, because neither alone is reliable:

  • player.soul:IsInCombatDanger() is what vanilla scripts use, but it

    • reflects immediate danger rather than "a fight is happening", and it was observed reading false through a whole session the player described as being in combat with guards. Mounted and moving, the player may simply not be in danger at the instant of each impact.

  • The victim having a weapon drawn. Townsfolk do not walk around armed, so

    • this catches the case the first signal misses: charging someone who is actively fighting.

Both raw values are logged so a disagreement between them is visible rather than being hidden behind a single boolean.

npctablevictim entity

ThrowRider(horseEnt, playerEnt)

Throws the rider from the horse.

Prefers the horse's own RearAndThrowDown, which plays the animation of the animal rearing and unseating its rider. Falls back to ragdolling the player, which is what earlier builds did and which reads as the player simply collapsing rather than being thrown.

RearAndThrowDown is undocumented. It sits on the horse entity's horse extension, alongside HasRider and IsMountable.

SafeUpdate()

One tick of collision detection.

Bails out early unless the player is mounted and moving at least at walking pace, then tests every entity within HitRadius of the horse.

Every engine call is wrapped in pcall, because entities can be unstreamed or partially initialized at any moment and an uncaught error would kill the timer loop for the rest of the session.

UpdateTimer(assignedTick)

Reschedules itself every 100 ms and runs one detection tick.

The tick number guards against duplicate loops. Each load screen starts a new loop, and any loop whose number no longer matches the current one stops on its next iteration, so reloading a save leaves no stale timers running.

ParameterTypeDescription
assignedTicknumberthe loop generation this timer belongs to
